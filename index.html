Namma Web Advanced AI Research Labs — Namma Web LLP

Headquartered in Karnataka with branches across Bengaluru (Peenya) and Nelamangala, Namma Web is a pioneering DPIIT-recognized startup and ISO 21001:2018 certified educational technology research institution. We bridge the gap between academic theory and production-grade software engineering through hands-on AI research, local Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architectures, and structured institutional skill training programs.
